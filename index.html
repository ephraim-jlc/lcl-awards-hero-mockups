How to read the mockups

  • Each page opens with annotations on. Yellow labels mark the four hero fields: Hero Title, Hero Text, Primary CTA and Secondary CTA. A CTA left empty is hidden.
  • Use the toggle at the top of a page to switch annotations off and see the clean design.
  • Developer notes at the bottom of each page cover the implications, including the removed section index.
  • Photos are placeholders. Body copy is taken from the live pages where it was available.
